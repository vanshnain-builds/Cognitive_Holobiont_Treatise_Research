# Cognitive Holobiont Research

Rigorous research dossier for the **Cognitive Holobiont Treatise**.

## Current milestone

**Milestone 01 — Literature + Mathematical Audit**

The Treatise is being evaluated as a conceptual distributed modular-intelligence architecture. Major claims are classified as **established**, **plausible engineering synthesis**, **unsupported/speculative**, or **mathematically incorrect/incomplete**. No implementation or validation claim is made without evidence.

### Key findings so far

- Specialist modular computation, sparse MoE routing, learned communication, hypernetworks, continual learning, uncertainty estimation, and fault-tolerant distributed systems have strong prior foundations.
- The complete Holobiont architecture is a **research synthesis**, not an established intelligence paradigm.
- KL divergence cannot be applied directly to arbitrary hidden vectors; latent spaces and discrepancy measures must be explicitly typed.
- Spectral-gap results require specific consensus dynamics and graph assumptions; they do not establish a general “speed of thought” law.
- The Treatise's Lyapunov/self-healing proof sketch is incomplete and requires a formal stochastic/time-varying dynamical system plus stability assumptions.
- Hypernetworks can generate weights, but this does not establish faithful regeneration of arbitrary organs.
- Entropy alone is insufficient as a corruption detector; calibration, OOD detection, disagreement and provenance should be evaluated jointly.
- Federated/distributed gradients are not automatically private.
- “Immortality,” zero downtime, fixed regeneration percentages, and economic/availability targets remain hypotheses until experimentally measured.
